    How this age is calculated

    1. Compare the birth date with the selected target date.
    2. Count complete calendar years and months.
    3. Count the remaining calendar days and convert the total.

    The calculator uses real calendar lengths and does not treat every month as 30 days. Under the fixed-calendar-anchor rule, February 28 to March 31 is counted as 1 month and 3 days.
